PhD Student, Dynamical Neuroscience — University of California, Santa Barbara. Santa Barbara, CA · lgilnadolskis@ucsb.edu · Google Scholar

Research Interests

I work to improve the lives of people with vision loss through research in visual prosthetics, brain–computer interfaces, computer vision, and assistive technology — at the intersection of computer science, biomedical engineering, and neuroscience, with a focus on the real-world usability and safety of cortical and retinal implants for blind users.

  • M.S. in Computational Biomedical Engineering, Carnegie Mellon University — Advisor: Prof. Matthew Smith. Thesis: Exploring top-down visual pathways using micro-stimulation and its applications to cortical visual prosthesis. 2023.
  • B.S. in Computer Science, Minor in Neuroscience, University of Minnesota, Twin Cities. 2021.
  • Study Abroad, Queen Mary University of London — computer science and neuroscience. 2018.
